Assessment guide

New concept: an idea absent from the prior synthesis. New relationship: a proposed connection absent from it. Boundary refinement: a change to where a claim applies. Measurement refinement: a change in how the construct is observed. Challenge: evidence conflicting with a prior claim. Confirmation: support without a substantive addition. Unresolved: the contribution cannot yet be determined.

Select the primary contribution and explain any secondary contributions in the rationale. These are judgments about this project’s prior evidence, not claims of novelty across all scholarship. Confidence concerns your judgment; it is not a calibrated probability.
