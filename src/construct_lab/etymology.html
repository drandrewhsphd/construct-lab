Start here · how to use this workspace
  1. Open a Historical source and follow its source link.
  2. Open an Interpretation to inspect the inferential bridge and alternative reading.
  3. Compare Candidate definitions and their boundaries.
  4. Inspect Proposed relationships for mechanisms, predictions and tests.
  5. Use Boundary cases and Assessments to record judgments. Development examples are not independent validation.

This version supports curated source entry and traceable human/AI-assisted reasoning. It does not automatically search historical archives or generate validated theory. No fit score or saturation claim is computed from pilot entries.

Evaluation runs

Each run preserves its starting protocol version. Demonstration observations are synthetic; empirical observations require actual data. This local workspace records reviewer exposure but does not enforce blinding or authenticate identities.

Revision tracking

Compare saved candidate versions and inspect which earlier judgments need reassessment. A proposed revision does not invalidate a judgment under its original definition or approve the new candidate.

Evidence-task tracking

Open a task to inspect its question and search plan. Add documented findings through Record type, link them to the task, and record a decision. Resolving a task does not revise or approve a definition automatically. Searches are planned here, not run automatically.

Boundary-case testing

Click a judgment to inspect its rationale and evidence needed. Included means the case meets that candidate definition; it does not mean the definition is correct. These definitions describe different properties and levels of analysis.

Development judgments are provisional, not independent validation. Stale judgments are displayed but excluded from current summaries. Multiple judgments are retained.
