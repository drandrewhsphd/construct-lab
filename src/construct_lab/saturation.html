Configure criteria

The initial values are illustrative design defaults, not validated scientific thresholds. Record why your chosen criteria fit the project. Saving preserves previous configurations in the history.

Novelty counts new concepts, relationships, refinements and challenges. Confirmations are non-novel. Unresolved findings block completeness. Each eligible passage must have a reviewed research assessment; draft and demonstration judgments do not count.

Documented evidence checks
